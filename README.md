# Minimalist Developer Portfolio

> Crafted with the dark minimalist aesthetic inspired by **xevrion.dev**, tailored with **Neon Green (`#00F801`)** and **Dark Aesthetic Green (`#0F460F`)**.

---

## 🎨 Theme Palette
- **Light / Neon Green**: `#00F801` (RGB: `0, 248, 1`)
- **Dark Aesthetic Green**: `#0F460F` (RGB: `15, 70, 15`)
- **Deep Background**: `#050805` / `#0a0f0a`

---

## 🚀 Sections Included (In Requested Order)
1. **Header & Profile**:
   - Avatar with status badge + live online ring
   - Name & headline
   - Location & live digital clock (updates dynamically every second!)
   - Bio with subtle hover links
   - Fast Action Buttons (`Resume`, `Book a call`, copy email button with toast notification)
   - Social links (`GitHub`, `X`, `LinkedIn`, `Sponsor`)
   - Shortcut hint (`Press Ctrl K to search the site`)
2. **Highlights**: Fast bullet facts & metrics with category tags.
3. **Work (Positions)**: Expandable interactive accordion with roles, impact bullets, company tags, and tech stack badges.
4. **Skills**: Placed directly after Work — categorized into Languages, Frontend & UI, Backend & Systems, Cloud & Tools with neon hover chips.
5. **Projects**: Clean minimalist cards with GitHub stars (`★`), language tags, live indicators, and links.
6. **Open Source (OSS)**: Merged pull requests list with repo tags, dates, and an expandable toggle (`Show all 78 pull requests`).
7. **Hackathons & Competitions**: Placed after OSS — event names, trophy badges, project descriptions, and Devpost/GitHub links.
8. **Achievements & Certifications**: Placed after Hackathons — cloud certifications, hackathon wins, competitive programming milestones.
9. **Contributions Calendar**: 52-week activity heatmap utilizing Neon Green and Dark Aesthetic Green heat levels with hover tooltips.
10. **Command Palette (`Ctrl+K` / `⌘K`)**: Interactive Spotlight / Raycast modal for instant search navigation across projects, sections, actions, and skills.

---

## 🛠️ How to Customize with Your Details
All information is centralized in **`data.js`**:
1. Open `data.js`.
2. Replace:
   - `personal`: Name, role, avatar URL, location, email, social URLs.
   - `highlights`: Your key milestones.
   - `work`: Your jobs, internships, or leadership positions.
   - `skills`: Your tech stack.
   - `projects`: Your projects with stars, descriptions, and URLs.
   - `openSource`: Your pull requests and contributions.
   - `hackathons`: Your hackathon wins and submissions.
   - `achievementsAndCerts`: Your certifications and awards.

Or simply send your details in chat, and I will plug them in for you!
